\section{Stakeholder Analysis}
Primary stakeholders include: (i)~hydroponic farmers, (ii)~agri-entrepreneurs, (iii)~system administrators, (iv)~project supervisors, and (v)~cloud service providers. Farmers demand low-latency recommendations and bilingual clarity. Administrators curate knowledge-base documents and API keys.

\section{Feasibility Study}
\textbf{Technical feasibility} is established through Flutter cross-platform tooling, mature Firebase SDKs, and scikit-learn training on commodity hardware. \textbf{Economic feasibility} leverages free-tier Firebase and Render for academic prototypes. \textbf{Operational feasibility} depends on farmer smartphone penetration and intermittent LTE connectivity; offline fallbacks mitigate risk.
